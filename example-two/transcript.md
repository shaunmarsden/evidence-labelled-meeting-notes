# Fictional Transcript: Hiring Panel Debrief

Overton Labs, an entirely fictional company. Debrief after final interviews for a Senior Support Engineer role. Participants: Casey Blake (Hiring Manager), Delia Lockwood (Panel, Engineering), Piers Lowther (Panel, Ops).

---

**Casey:** Let's start with Jamie Ellis. Delia, you ran the technical round, how did that go?

**Delia:** Strong. Genuinely one of the best technical rounds we've had this quarter. No gaps I'd flag.

**Piers:** Agreed from my side too, the ops scenario went well, good instincts under pressure.

**Casey:** Great, so are we all agreed, we extend an offer to Jamie?

**Delia:** Yes, from me.

**Piers:** Yes, no hesitation.

**Casey:** Good, that's decided then. I'll get the offer out to Jamie by Friday.

**Delia:** Sounds good.

**Casey:** Now, Sasha Rowe. Piers, you had some concerns?

**Piers:** I did. The ops scenario was fine, but there was a gap on the incident escalation question that worried me a bit. Not a dealbreaker necessarily, but I noticed it.

**Delia:** I saw that too on my side, actually. Technical fundamentals were solid otherwise.

**Casey:** Okay. Given that's come up from both of you independently, I don't think we should decide today either way. Let's get a second technical interview scheduled specifically probing incident response, then decide after that.

**Piers:** That makes sense to me.

**Delia:** Agreed, that's the right call.

**Casey:** Good, I'll own getting that second interview scheduled, I'll aim for early next week.

**Casey:** Last thing, and this one's more open, should we start doing take-home technical tests for all candidates going forward, not just live interviews?

**Delia:** Could be worth trying at some point. Might help with exactly this kind of gap.

**Piers:** Maybe, though it adds time to the process. Not sure it's worth it for every role.

**Casey:** Let's leave that one as a maybe for now, not something we're actually changing yet. Thanks both, that's everything for today.
