# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before treating the write-up as the record of the meeting.

- [ ] Every confirmed fact was actually stated directly in the meeting, not inferred
- [ ] Every action has an owner and timing, or is honestly marked unknown rather than guessed
- [ ] Anything that sounds like a decision but was not actually agreed is flagged as a non-decision, not written up as one
- [ ] A genuine, unambiguous decision was recorded plainly as a decision, not hedged for the sake of caution
- [ ] Any conflict between two things said in the meeting was surfaced, not silently resolved in one side's favour
- [ ] No personal or confidential information beyond what the write-up actually needs was included
